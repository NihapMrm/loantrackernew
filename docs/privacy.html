Privacy Policy — LoanTracker

Last updated: September 30, 2026

LoanTracker ("the app") is a personal loan-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Data we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and account ID from that provider.
  • Loan records you enter: amounts, dates, counterparties, notes, and repayment status that you manually add to track loans.

Data we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers — the app shows no ads.
  • No analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • No health, location, or contacts data.

Biometric lock

If you enable app-lock, biometric verification (fingerprint/face) is handled entirely by your device's operating system. No biometric data is sent to us or stored on our servers.

How your data is stored

Your account and loan data are stored using Supabase, our backend data provider, which processes data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Some data may also be cached locally on your device for offline use.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data is only shared with the service providers above as needed to operate the app.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

Contact
