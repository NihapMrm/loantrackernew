Privacy Policy — LoanTracker

Last updated: October 4, 2026

LoanTracker ("the app") is a personal loan-tracking app that works entirely offline. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Summary

All data you enter stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers, and makes no network requests. We cannot see your data.

Data stored on your device

  • People you add: name, optional phone number and notes.
  • Loans and payments you record: amounts, dates, notes, repayment status.
  • Accounts you create to group loans.
  • App preferences (currency, date format, pinned loans) and, if you enable app-lock, a one-way hash of your PIN.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes it.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information is sent to us or to any third party.
  • No advertising identifiers; the app shows no ads.
  • No analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, health or sign-in data.

Biometric lock

If you enable app-lock, biometric verification (fingerprint/face) is handled entirely by your device's operating system. The app only receives a success or failure result. No biometric data is stored or transmitted.

Backups

You can export your data as a JSON file and import it later. The file is created on your device and shared only to the location you choose in the system share sheet. Keep backup files safe; they are not encrypted.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data, because we never receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Data deletion

Use Settings → Clear All Data, or uninstall the app. Because no data is held by us, there is nothing to delete on any server.
